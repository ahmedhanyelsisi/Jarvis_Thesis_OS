# Jarvis Thesis OS Vision

## Mission

Jarvis Thesis OS is an AI-powered research assistant designed to support researchers through the complete academic workflow.

## Core Philosophy

Jarvis does not replace the researcher.

Jarvis enhances human research capability by providing intelligent assistance, automation, and organization.

## Main Capabilities

- Academic writing assistance
- Literature analysis
- LaTeX automation
- Citation management
- Research visualization
- Thesis review
- Defense preparation

## Architecture Principles

1. Modular AI agents
2. Human-in-the-loop decision making
3. Clean separation between thesis data and AI system
4. Secure handling of research information
5. Extensible architecture

## Long-Term Vision

Transform Jarvis Thesis OS into a complete AI Research Operating System.
