# DEFERRED_VOICE_HARDWARE_ACCEPTANCE

**Deferred from:** Stone 26/26.5 software freeze  
**Required before:** Stone 30 final product acceptance

The voice software path is integrated and security-hardened, but it is not
qualified for real microphone use or hands-free operation. A successful final
acceptance must demonstrate, through the production HUD and without retaining
raw audio:

1. real microphone capture;
2. local STT;
3. Conversation Engine routing;
4. a supported JARVIS workflow;
5. local TTS; and
6. audible user-confirmed playback.

Record selected device IDs, expected and recognized text, latency, workflow
classification, playback confirmation, and pass/fail. Wake remains disabled or
experimental until separately qualified. This requirement does not grant voice
authorization or autonomous capability.
